# Instructions for AI assistants

The rules for any assistant that works on this repository (Codex, ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude or others) live in **[CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)**: commands, structure, product rules, conventions and known traps. Read it first: it applies to you too.

Before you start:

1. Read **[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md)**: what is in progress, what comes next and what has been decided against. Work on what is there or on what the owner asks; put a new idea in its "Idee" list instead of starting it.
2. What the app must do is in **[docs/specs/requisiti.md](docs/specs/requisiti.md)** (in Italian); how it is built is in [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).
3. Every pull request follows the checklist in [.github/pull_request_template.md](.github/pull_request_template.md).

The owner tries visible or audible changes on a phone before they are merged. Never commit signing keys, `android/key.properties` or other secrets.
