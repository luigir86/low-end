Open audio files from other apps, such as WhatsApp, with Low End and study them in the Player. Sharing a take now sends it as a file, so WhatsApp keeps its name. A phone call pauses the metronome, Harmony and the Player, and recording from the phone's microphone leaves Bluetooth headsets in music mode. The X in the track field discards a file or a take (with Undo). Small fixes to the tuner, Harmony and loops.
